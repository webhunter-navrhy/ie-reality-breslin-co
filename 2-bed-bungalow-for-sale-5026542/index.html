Description

Attractive end-of-terrace bungalow in a private purpose-built retirement complex, located in Beechfield Manor, c.200m to the north of Shankill Village, off Shanganagh Road. Within easy reach of Shankill Village, Shopping Centre, churches, and the beach. Multiple bus-routes & Shankill DART station is c. 500m. Floor area of c. 57.60 sq.m. / 620 sq.ft. Accommodation includes entrance lobby, lounge, separate kitchen, two bedrooms and bathroom. Double-glazed uPVC windows. Electric storage & convector heating. Communal gardens & communal parking. Over 50s only.
